Privacy Policy — Tap N Go

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Tap N Go ("the app") is a point-of-sale and inventory app for small retail shops, developed by Wimosoft. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

Data we collect

  • Account details: name, email address and role (Admin or Cashier), so staff can sign in.
  • Shop records: products, categories, stock levels, sales, refunds, shifts and store settings that the shop's staff enter.
  • Images: product photos and profile pictures that users choose to upload.
  • Access requests: the business name and contact details submitted through the "Request Access" form.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan barcodes and take product photos. Scans are processed on the device and are not recorded.

How it is used

This data is used only to run the app: signing in, processing sales, tracking inventory and producing the shop's own reports. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, our backend provider, and is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Unsent sales may be kept temporarily on the device until the connection returns.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the shop's account is active. To have an account or its data deleted, email asimglobal9@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

Deleting your Tap N Go account

To delete your Tap N Go account and its data:

  1. Email asimglobal9@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Tap N Go account".
  2. Include the name of your shop. A shop admin can also make the request for a staff member.
  3. We confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Deleted: your sign-in, your profile picture, and the name and email address on your profile. If you sent a "Request Access" form, its contact details are deleted too.

Kept: sales, refunds, shifts and stock records you entered belong to the shop's business records, so they are kept for the shop, linked to an anonymous placeholder instead of your name. A shop admin who wants all of the shop's data removed can ask for that in the same email.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the policy changes and revise the date above.
